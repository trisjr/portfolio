GLA connects Vietnamese technical candidates with domestic and international companies, focused on semiconductors, chip design, AI, automation and IT. Phase 1 covers the core MVP — recruiting, candidate profiles, administration and a brokerage Kanban — delivered by a 1–2 developer team working alongside AI agents in a spec-driven monorepo. The project is in its planning and requirements stage, with BRD/PRD, architecture decisions and a weekly delivery plan in place ahead of bootstrapping.
